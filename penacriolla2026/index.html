About the event

Peña Criolla is a traditional Peruvian celebration of food, music and culture. Oslo con Ají brings the flavours of Lima to the heart of Oslo — authentic dishes prepared with Peruvian ingredients and served with the warmth of Peruvian hospitality.

The event coincides with Peru's national holidays on 28 July, and is an opportunity to taste some of the most beloved dishes from Peruvian culinary tradition.
